OWNER RULES: standing rules for every custom client site
Set by the owner. They apply to every session that works in custom-sites or on a
client's store. Newest first. "Proposed" rules are drafts: follow them, but they
are not agreed until the owner moves them up into the numbered list.

------------------------------------------------------------
2026-11-24
------------------------------------------------------------
1. NEVER PUSH CUSTOM-SITES TO GITHUB.
   The repo is local only. Commits are fine, pushes are not, and there is no
   remote. The owner says if and when that changes.

2. A CUSTOM SITE RUNS ON THE CLIENT'S OWN GLOWPLY STORE.
   Breakdance + WooCommerce + Arabic RTL, the same stack and build flow as the
   templates. Anything that would need another platform is a question for the
   owner before work starts, not a decision a session makes.

3. CLIENT MATERIAL STAYS OUT OF GIT.
   Contracts, invoices, phone numbers, logins, the client's original photos
   and anything they sent in confidence go in clients/<slug>/private/, which
   .gitignore keeps out of commits. Commit the brief, the brand notes, the
   copy, the mockups and the review log, never the raw material.
   Never put client details in a request to an outside service (a stock-photo
   search, an image model, a URL).

4. A CUSTOM SITE CAN BECOME A TEMPLATE, BUT ONLY THROUGH TEMPLATE-GUIDELINE.
   Optional, and only with the client's OK where the design was made for them.
   It is rebuilt there under template-guideline's rules (5 palette roles,
   approved fonts, the theme/demo split, the real-store check, an inactive feed
   entry, the owner's sign-off), with the client's brand, name and photos
   taken out. See PROCESS.md, "Promote to a template".

------------------------------------------------------------
Proposed (2026-11-24, not agreed yet)
------------------------------------------------------------
P1. THE CLIENT SIGNS OFF, IN WRITING, BEFORE LAUNCH.
    Their OK goes in SIGN-OFF.md with the date and what they approved (the
    page list and the review round). Nothing goes live on their domain before.

P2. TWO REVIEW ROUNDS ARE INCLUDED.
    Each round is one list of changes from the client, logged in REVIEWS.md.
    A third round is extra work and is agreed before it starts.

P3. THE CLIENT'S OWN PHOTOS FIRST, THEN STOCK, THEN GENERATED.
    Their photos first. Stock (Pexels, Pixabay) for the gaps. Generated images
    (fal.ai, as in template-guideline's PHOTOS.md) only when stock has nothing
    and the client has agreed to generated images on their site. No paid image
    generation for mockups.

P4. EVERY CLAIM ON THE SITE IS TRUE FOR THIS CLIENT.
    Delivery times, returns, cash on delivery, ratings, "handmade", "since
    2015": each one comes from the brief or the client, not from the design.
    No fake urgency, no invented counts (base-kit's honesty rules).

P5. NEVER TOUCH A LIVE CLIENT STORE WITHOUT SAYING WHAT WILL CHANGE.
    Once a store is live, build changes on a copy or a draft page, show the
    client, then swap. Never delete a client's products, orders, pages or media.

P6. CHECK EVERY PAGE LOGGED OUT AT 1440, 390 AND 320 BEFORE EACH REVIEW ROUND.
    With the checks in SCORECARD.md, screenshots in the client's mockups/ or
    review folder.
