# Brand: «مرسى» Marsa

> Virtual client (see BRIEF.md). These are the "client's" existing brand assets, as they'd describe them.

## Logo

- What they have: a round rubber stamp they press on every kraft bag in navy ink. The word «مرسى» in the middle, «محمصة بن · إسكندرية» around the edge, and a small anchor under the name. No digital file: it gets redrawn as an SVG for the site (content/logo/, the direction stage).
- Versions needed: the full round stamp; the word «مرسى» on its own for the header; navy on kraft and kraft on navy.
- Rule from the client: "the stamp looks stamped, not printed". The ink is a little uneven and the edge isn't perfect.

## Colours

The client's own, from their bags and stickers. No palette limit; the direction may add neutrals.

| Colour | Hex | Used for (on the bags) |
|---|---|---|
| Kraft | #C9A77C | The bag paper |
| Stamp navy | #1F2A44 | The stamp and all printed text |
| Date-sticker red | #B5452F | The round roast-date sticker, nothing else |
| Paper white | #F4EFE6 | The label paper inside the bag window |

**Brand rule:** red is the roast date's colour. It marks freshness (a roast date, the next roast day), never a sale or a warning.

## Fonts

They have none ("we choose"). The direction picks them, as long as:
- the Arabic reads well at 16 px on a phone;
- it sits well next to a hand-pressed stamp, so nothing cold or corporate.
Free fonts only (Google Fonts).

## Voice

- **How they talk:** Alexandrian Egyptian Arabic, warm, a little teasing, like the man at the counter. Short sentences.
- **Words they use:** طازة، تحميصة، طحنة، ريحة، "اسألنا"، "على مزاجك".
- **Words they avoid:** فاخر، حصري، "الأفضل"، "لا تفوّت"؛ and English coffee jargon without saying what it means («أسيدية» needs a word next to it: «حموضة خفيفة زي الفاكهة»).

## Photos

- **What they have:** nothing usable yet (phone photos of bags on the counter, in private/ when the client sends them).
- **What we use for the trial:** stock only (Pixabay, Pexels), one look: daylight, kraft, wood, white walls, beans and cups, no people's faces, no visible brands. No generated images (proposed rule P3: the client hasn't agreed to them).
