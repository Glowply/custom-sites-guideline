# Brief: «مرسى» Marsa, a coffee roaster in Alexandria

> **Virtual client.** Marsa is made up, to run the custom-site process end to end (2026-11-25). Every fact below is the "client's" answer for this trial; none is about a real business. The owner plays the client at the review stages. For now the site is built on a glowplylibrary.com subsite instead of a client store.

**Status:** direction
**Store:** for the trial, a glowplylibrary.com subsite (no feed entry: this is not a template)
**Deadline:** none (trial)

## Must know

- **What they sell:** roasted coffee, whole bean or ground to order, plus two brewing tools. 9 products at launch, EGP 200–1,450.
- **Who sells it:** a small family roaster: one roaster, one shop counter, started selling online on Instagram two years ago. Wants their own site because Instagram orders get lost in DMs.
- **Who buys:**
  - Alexandrians 25–50 who drink Turkish coffee at home every day and want it fresher than the supermarket's.
  - Younger buyers (20–35) who've started making V60 or espresso at home and want to know what they're buying.
  - Small cafés and offices that buy by the kilo.
  Mostly on phones, from Instagram and WhatsApp links.
- **Country and currency:** Egypt, EGP.
- **Payment:** cash on delivery, and card online.
- **Roasting and shipping:**
  - They roast twice a week, on Sunday and Wednesday, and only roast what's been ordered plus the counter's stock.
  - Every bag has a sticker with its roast date.
  - Orders go out the day after the next roast.
  - Alexandria: delivered the day it goes out, EGP 50. Other governorates: courier, 2–4 days after it goes out, EGP 85.
- **Grind:** every coffee comes whole or ground: Turkish (very fine), espresso, filter (V60 and drip), French press. Ground at order, not before.
- **Returns:** unopened bags within 7 days of delivery. Ground coffee can't be returned, unless we ground it wrong; then we replace it.
- **WhatsApp:** yes, for questions and for café orders. Number in private/ (not set for the trial).
- **Pages:**
  - home, shop, product, cart, checkout;
  - «المحمصة» (about the roaster);
  - «إزاي تعمل قهوتك» (brew guides: Turkish, V60, espresso, French press);
  - «للكافيهات والمكاتب» (by the kilo, with an inquiry form);
  - policies, contact.
- **What the site must do that a template doesn't:**
  1. A grind choice on every coffee.
  2. The next roast day and when an order will go out, said plainly where people decide.
  3. Brew guides that link to the coffees that suit each method.
  4. A café and office inquiry form.
  5. Their existing stamp-and-kraft brand.

## Taste

- **They like:** their own bags (kraft paper, navy stamp, the red date sticker); old Alexandrian shop signs; the feeling of a counter where someone explains the coffee to you.
- **They don't like:**
  - black-and-gold "luxury coffee" sites;
  - anything that looks like a chain;
  - coffee-snob jargon without an explanation;
  - fake "limited stock" banners.
- **Their words for how it should feel:** طازة، قريب، واضح، إسكندراني، من غير فذلكة (fresh, close, clear, Alexandrian, no showing off).

## Notes

The cardamom Turkish blend («محوّج») outsells everything; the client wants it first on the home page. The single origins are the reason younger buyers come; they want the origin, the roast level and two or three taste notes on each, and nothing more.
