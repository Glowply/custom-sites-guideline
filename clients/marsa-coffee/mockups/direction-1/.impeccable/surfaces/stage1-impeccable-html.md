# Surface brief: Marsa home page, direction 1 «حيطة الأدراج»

Scope: the home page mockup (one of three client-facing directions). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: Alexandrian Turkish-coffee drinkers 25–50, home V60/espresso beginners 20–35, small cafés; on phones, from Instagram/WhatsApp, patchy data.
Job: believe the coffee is fresh (next roast day, roast date on the bag), pick a coffee by brew method, pick a grind, add to cart. Proof is the roast rule and the client's own facts only (BRIEF.md "Claims we can make").
Constraints: Arabic RTL, one self-contained HTML, stock photos from ../../content/photos only, mocked "today" = Wed 25 Nov 2 pm, fake cart OK. Red only for roast dates.
Memorable moment: a drawer slides out of the navy wall and a kraft cone fills as you pick a grind.
Unresolved: real bag photos and the redrawn stamp SVG come later from the client.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is the attar's wall of labelled drawers behind Marsa's counter; you pick a coffee by how you brew, its drawer slides out, and it is ground for you. Refuses the dark bean hero with "shop now" over a grid of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Painted stamp-navy drawer fronts (#1F2A44, ~50% of the page) with drawer-gap navy #141C30 seams; tin pulls #A9ADB3; enamel paper-white plates #F4EFE6 with Aref Ruqaa labels; kraft #C9A77C labels, cones, wrapping and the primary button; walnut counter #4B3323; red #B5452F only on roast-date numerals and the strip sticker. Readex Pro body and numerals. An open drawer is a tray: notes, weight, price, five tin-scoop grind chips, add to cart. No brass, no gilt, no black-and-gold.

STORY: The visitor sees the next roast day first (Sunday 29 Nov, order by Sat 11 pm, out Mon 30), recognises a counter not a chain, picks a drawer by brew method, reads its roast date, picks a grind, adds to cart, sees how the order travels (تطلب، نحمّص، يوصلك), and that help is on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 390: navy roast strip with «الأحد ٢٩ نوفمبر» on a red round sticker; header with 40 px stamp, wordmark, cart; headline on a kraft label pinned above the wall (Ruqaa 34 px) with the lead; a 2×2 block of navy method drawers, each plate showing its grind line; the كنكة drawer half open on المحوّج with photo crop, 230 ج, grind preset تركي ناعم جدًا and a full-width kraft add-to-cart, all above the fold. 1440: right 40% (start side) holds headline, lead, «اختار بنّك», «إزاي تعمل قهوتك»; left 60% is the wall, four method columns with the coffees that suit each; a full-width walnut counter strip at the bottom with the open المحوّج tray resting on it.

FORM: Drawer Wall, grounded candidate #4 of 7, assigned by the roll. seed:058449b6c0e7cfcb. Signature: a tapped drawer slides out toward you and its neighbours shift aside; picking a grind fills a kraft cone with a tin scoop; one axis only, out of the wall and back, 240 ms ease-out, no bounce; reduced motion swaps states instantly. Raises: fixed order stations with the Saturday 11 pm cutoff as a line on the counter (darkroom); one continuous wall, no pop-ups (ebru); every label a lockup legible at 120 px that becomes the cart line (deck wall); light-to-dark roast inside each column (deep dive); a batch card with the red roast date in the same spot on every drawer (kiln shelf). Section order: strip, header, hero+wall, full wall (7 coffees, tools on a top shelf), order stations on the counter, brew teaser on a cone wrapper, cafés (sack drawer), help at the counter, footer stamp. Honest risk: coffees suit several methods; show each coffee once and use methods as filters.
