# Directions: «مرسى»

From Impeccable's direction round on 2026-11-25 (new world, Persuade surface, home page). The build is code-led because there's no image generation. Three clearly different directions were chosen for the client. Each one is built as a mockup in `direction-1/`, `direction-2/` and `direction-3/`.

Seven grounded directions, ranked:
1. tear-off wall calendar
2. old Alexandrian shop signboards
3. the counter conversation as a WhatsApp thread
4. the attar's drawer wall
5. the Raml tram timetable
6. a harbour cargo manifest
7. vintage Egyptian chromolithograph labels

The roll assigned #4.

Dealt challengers:
- **Screenprint overlap:** competitive.
- **Darkroom:** declined. It needs a countdown, which is fake urgency.
- **Ebru, skate deck wall, mesophotic dive, kiln shelf:** declined, but each lent one discipline to direction 1 (see its raises).

---

## 1. Drawer Wall «حيطة الأدراج»

**Source:** assigned (grounded #4), seed:058449b6c0e7cfcb

**Thesis:** The home page is the wall of drawers behind Marsa's counter. The model is the عطّار (attar) who weighs and grinds for you. Each coffee sits in a labelled drawer. You pick it by how you brew, and it opens to be ground for you. This refuses the usual dark bean hero with a "shop now" button over a grid of equal cards.

**Own world:**
- Palette:
  - Stamp navy #1F2A44 for painted drawer fronts (about 50% of the page).
  - Kraft #C9A77C for paper labels, cones, wrapping and the primary button.
  - Paper white #F4EFE6 for enamel label plates and reading panels.
  - Date red #B5452F only for roast-date numerals and the strip's sticker.
  - Added: walnut counter #4B3323, drawer-gap navy #141C30, tin pulls #A9ADB3.
- Materials: painted wood, tin pulls, enamel plates, a kraft cone, the stamp pressed on labels.
- Components: a drawer is a product tile. An open drawer is a tray with the notes, weight, price, five tin-scoop grind chips and add to cart.
- Type: Aref Ruqaa for labels and headings. Readex Pro for body and numerals.
- Translations from the attar look: brass and gilt become tin and kraft, so nothing reads as black and gold. The attar's green becomes navy.

**Story:** The visitor sees the next roast day first, and recognises a counter, not a chain. They pick a drawer by brew method, read its roast date, pick a grind and add to cart. They see how the order travels, and that help is on WhatsApp.

**First viewport, 390 wide:**
- A navy roast strip, with «الأحد ٢٩ نوفمبر» on a red round sticker.
- Header: stamp (40 px), wordmark, cart.
- The headline on a kraft label pinned above the wall (Ruqaa, 34 px), with the lead under it.
- A 2×2 block of navy method drawers, each plate showing its grind line.
- The كنكة drawer half open on المحوّج: photo crop, 230 ج, grind preset to تركي ناعم جدًا, and a full-width kraft add-to-cart button. All of this sits above the fold.

**First viewport, 1440 wide:**
- The right 40% (the start side in RTL) holds the headline, lead, «اختار بنّك» and «إزاي تعمل قهوتك».
- The left 60% is the wall: four method columns with the coffees that suit each.
- A walnut counter strip runs full width along the bottom, with the open المحوّج tray resting on it.

**Signature:** A tapped drawer slides out toward you and its neighbours shift aside. Picking a grind fills a kraft cone with a tin scoop. Motion moves on one axis only, out of the wall and back, at 240 ms ease-out with no bounce. Reduced motion swaps the states instantly.

**Section order:**
1. Roast strip
2. Header
3. Hero and wall (hero, best seller and methods merged)
4. The full wall (7 coffees, tools on a top shelf)
5. Order stations on the counter
6. Brew teaser on a cone wrapper
7. Cafés (the by-the-kilo sack drawer)
8. Help at the counter («اسألنا» on WhatsApp)
9. Footer stamp

**Honest risk:** One coffee can suit several methods, so the wall can turn into a confusing matrix. Show each coffee once and use the methods as filters. The bean photos also look alike.

**Raises (one discipline borrowed from each challenger):**
- From the darkroom: the order runs as fixed stations (تطلب، نحمّص، يوصلك). The Saturday 11 pm cutoff is a line on the counter, said where people decide, with no countdown.
- From ebru: the wall is one continuous surface. Opening a drawer pushes its neighbours aside, with no pop-up window.
- From the deck wall: every coffee label is a lockup that still reads at 120 px. The same label becomes the cart line.
- From the deep dive: one axis rules the wall. Inside each column, drawers run from light roast to dark, so a drawer's position says its roast.
- From the kiln shelf: every drawer has a batch card with its roast date in red numerals, in the same spot on every drawer.

## 2. Roast Calendar «نتيجة التحميص»

**Source:** grounded #1

**Thesis:** The page is the tear-off wall calendar shops hand out, with their name printed on the backboard. The date is the hero, not the beans. This refuses freshness as a small-print claim under a bean photo.

**Own world:**
- Palette:
  - Stamp navy #1F2A44 for the printed backboard (the ground).
  - Kraft #C9A77C for cardboard edges and the primary button.
  - Paper white #F4EFE6 for the newsprint sheet block: perforated, stapled, with a punched hang hole.
  - Date red #B5452F for the roast-day numerals, and for Sundays and Wednesdays in the month grid. Calendars already print special days in red.
  - Added: newsprint grey #8C8A85.
- Materials: newsprint with slight offset misregistration and a tear edge.
- Components: cards are sheets, lists are month grids, and product panels are the backboard's printed ad boxes carrying the stamp.
- Type: Alexandria (900 for the giant numerals, 400/500 for body). Noto Naskh Arabic for the small lines.

**Story:** Today's roast has closed and the next one is Sunday the 29th, so the visitor knows exactly when their coffee is roasted and when it goes out. That makes "fresh and local" believable, and they add المحوّج right there.

**First viewport, 390 wide:**
- Strip and header.
- The sheet takes about 55% of the height: «الأحد», a red «٢٩» at about 170 px, «نوفمبر», «التحميصة الجاية», and in Naskh «اطلب لحد السبت ١١ بالليل · يطلع لك الإثنين ٣٠».
- On the navy backboard below: the headline, then a printed panel with المحوّج, 230 ج, a grind select and a kraft add-to-cart button.

**First viewport, 1440 wide:**
- The sheet block (520 px) hangs on the left.
- The headline, lead and «اختار بنّك» sit on the right.
- The المحوّج ad panel sits under the sheet, and the month grid peeks above the fold.

**Signature:** On load, today's sheet (Wednesday 25) curls and tears away to reveal Sunday 29. In the order section, sheets flip through the order day, roast day and out day. Motion is paper physics only (tear, curl, drop), in one direction, 400 ms, with no fades. Reduced motion shows a static sheet.

**Section order:**
1. Strip
2. Header
3. Sheet hero with the best seller
4. Methods as four ad panels
5. Coffees as a pad of sheets
6. How an order works as the month grid
7. Brew guide printed on the sheet's back, the way calendars print sayings
8. Cafés (a weekly order sheet)
9. Help
10. Footer (the backboard bottom with the stamp)

**Honest risk:** The dates must be computed live; if that fails, the hero lies. Shopping comes second to the calendar, and the giant numeral crowds a 390 px screen. It's also the most familiar of the seven.

## 3. Overprint «طبعة على الكرافت»

**Source:** challenger `ink-pigment-printmaking-screenprint-transparent-overlap` (competitive)

**Thesis:** The page is printed the way the bag is. Kraft is the paper, and navy and red are separate ink passes pulled in register. The stamp is one pass and the date sticker is another. This refuses the white e-commerce grid under a photo hero.

**Own world:**
- Palette:
  - Kraft #C9A77C as the field (about 70% of the page).
  - Stamp navy #1F2A44 as ink, with an overprint tone #2E2F3A where it crosses kraft.
  - Paper white #F4EFE6 for sticker labels and inputs, never as text on kraft (it fails contrast).
  - Date red #B5452F for the date sticker only.
  - Added: kraft-deep #A88658 for registration crosses and rules.
- Materials: kraft fibre and uneven rubber-stamp ink. Every photo is printed as a navy halftone duotone, which also makes the look-alike bean photos one set.
- Components:
  - Buttons are stamped ink blocks; pressed is a darker double impression.
  - The grind choice is five stamp marks, and the selected one is fully inked.
  - Product cards are white labels glued onto kraft.
- Type: Lalezar for headings, Almarai for body, Big Shoulders Stencil Display for Latin bits.
- Translation: only navy over kraft or navy over a photo may overlap. Red never mixes, because red means the date.

**Story:** The visitor recognises the bag they know from Instagram and understands that the date on it is real. They read how the order goes from roast to door, then add to cart.

**First viewport, 390 wide:**
- The strip is a white sticker band with a red «٢٩» sticker.
- Header.
- The Lalezar headline at 44 px over 3 lines, with the round stamp pressed half over its corner.
- The hero photo (the cooling tray) as a duotone on kraft, 200 px tall.
- A white المحوّج label overlapping the photo, with 230 ج, the grind stamps and a stamped add-to-cart button.

**First viewport, 1440 wide:**
- A kraft sheet with registration marks.
- The right half holds a 96 px headline, the lead and both links.
- The left half holds the duotone photo, with the stamp pressed across its edge and the المحوّج label glued at its corner.
- A banner sits on the fold: «تطلب ← نحمّص الأحد ٢٩ ← يطلع الإثنين».

**Signature:** Each section arrives as print passes: kraft, then navy (a 2 px ghost that settles into register), then the red sticker slapped on with a slight rotation. Add to cart stamps the label. Ink is either there or not, so there are no fades. Each pass takes 180 ms. Reduced motion shows the final print.

**Section order:**
1. Strip
2. Header
3. Poster hero with the best-seller label
4. Four duotone method tiles with their grind marks
5. Coffees as labels on kraft, light to dark
6. The order steps from roast to door
7. Brew guide as the insert card from a bag
8. Cafés (the sack duotone, «بالكيلو»)
9. Help
10. Footer with a large stamp

**Honest risk:** It's the closest to the brief's literal kraft-and-stamp picture, which is the rut. Lalezar on drenched kraft can read as loud, or as a hipster craft brand, against the client's «من غير فذلكة».
