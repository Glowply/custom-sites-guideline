Scope: Marsa home page, direction 3 mockup. Mode: Persuade. Arabic RTL, phone first (390), also 1440 and 320.

Audience and job: Alexandrian Turkish-coffee drinkers, new V60/espresso home brewers, small cafés. Believe the coffee is fresh and dated, pick by brew method, add to cart with a grind. Proof is the roast rule itself (next roast Sun 29 Nov, order by Sat 11 pm, out Mon 30 Nov). No reviews, counts, awards, discounts, urgency.

Constraints: copy from ../../content/HOME.md only; 17 stock photos by relative path, no new image files; red #B5452F only for roast dates; paper white never as text on kraft; lazy-load below the fold; slow 4G.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is printed the way the bag is: kraft is the paper, navy and red are separate ink passes pulled in register; the stamp is one pass, the date sticker another. Refuses the white e-commerce grid under a photo hero.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft #C9A77C field (~70%); stamp navy #1F2A44 ink with overprint #2E2F3A where it crosses kraft; paper white #F4EFE6 for sticker labels and inputs only; date red #B5452F for the date sticker only; kraft-deep #A88658 for registration crosses and rules. Kraft fibre, uneven rubber-stamp ink, every photo a navy halftone duotone. Buttons are stamped ink blocks (pressed = darker double impression); grind = five stamp marks, selected fully inked; product cards = white labels glued on kraft. Lalezar headings, Almarai body, Big Shoulders Stencil Display for Latin bits. Only navy overlaps kraft or photo; red never mixes.

STORY: The visitor recognises the bag they know from Instagram, understands the date on it is real, reads how the order goes from roast to door, then adds to cart.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 390: white sticker strip with red «٢٩» sticker; header; Lalezar headline 44px over 3 lines with the round stamp pressed half over its corner; hero cooling-tray photo as duotone on kraft, 200px tall; white المحوّج label overlapping the photo with 230 ج, grind stamps, stamped add-to-cart (primary action). 1440: kraft sheet with registration marks; right half 96px headline, lead, both links; left half duotone photo with the stamp across its edge and the المحوّج label glued at its corner; banner on the fold «تطلب ← نحمّص الأحد ٢٩ ← يطلع الإثنين».

SIGNATURE: Each section arrives as print passes: kraft, then navy (2px ghost settling into register), then the red sticker slapped on with slight rotation; add to cart stamps the label. No fades, 180ms per pass; reduced motion shows the final print.

FORM: challenger ink-pigment-printmaking-screenprint-transparent-overlap (competitive), seed: none (pinned) — "chosen for the client from the 2026-11-25 direction round (assigned seed:058449b6c0e7cfcb went to direction 1)".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Run note: the caller waived DESIGN.md and telemetry for this mockup; stage 2 (unslop) follows the finish review.
