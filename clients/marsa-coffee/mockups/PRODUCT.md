# PRODUCT.md — «مرسى» Marsa, a small coffee roaster in Alexandria

> Product context for Impeccable. Facts come from the client's brief (../BRIEF.md), brand (../BRAND.md) and content (../content/). Marsa is a virtual client for a process trial; treat it as real.

## What it is
A family coffee roaster in Alexandria: one roaster, one shop counter, selling on Instagram for two years and now moving to its own online store (on Glowply, Breakdance + WooCommerce, Arabic RTL). It sells Turkish coffee blends, three single origins, an espresso blend by the kilo, a copper cezve and a ceramic dripper. 9 products, EGP 210–1,450.

## Unique mechanism
Freshness you can see: they roast only twice a week (Sunday and Wednesday), grind each order to the buyer's brewing method, and stamp every bag with its roast date. The site tells you the next roast day and when your order goes out, before you buy.

## Audience and scene
- Alexandrians 25–50 who drink Turkish coffee daily and want it fresher than the supermarket's.
- Younger buyers 20–35 starting V60 or espresso at home, who want origin, roast and taste notes explained plainly.
- Small cafés and offices buying by the kilo.
On phones, from Instagram and WhatsApp, often on patchy mobile data. Arabic, right to left, Egyptian (Alexandrian) colloquial.

## What the home page must prove
1. This coffee is fresh: roasted this week, ground for me, dated.
2. I can pick the right coffee for how I make it (cezve, espresso, filter, French press) without knowing jargon.
3. Ordering is simple: cash on delivery or card, clear delivery days and costs, help on WhatsApp.
4. It's a real local roaster, not a chain and not a luxury brand.

## Product truths (never violate)
- Every claim comes from BRIEF.md's "Claims we can make". No reviews, ratings, awards, customer counts, "best", organic or fair trade, founding year, discounts or limited-stock banners.
- Roast days Sunday and Wednesday; ships the day after the roast; Alexandria same day once out (EGP 50), other governorates 2–4 days (EGP 85).
- Five grinds: whole bean, Turkish very fine, espresso, filter/V60, French press. Ground at order.
- Returns: unopened bags within 7 days; ground coffee only if they ground it wrong.

## Brand commitments (binding: the client's existing brand)
- **The round navy rubber stamp** with «مرسى» in the middle, «محمصة بن · إسكندرية» around the edge and a small anchor. It must look stamped, not printed.
- **Colours:** kraft #C9A77C, stamp navy #1F2A44, date-sticker red #B5452F, paper white #F4EFE6. Neutrals may be added.
- **Red means the roast date** (freshness: a roast date, the next roast day). Never a sale, a warning or a generic accent.
- Fonts are open (Google Fonts, Arabic reads well at 16 px on a phone, nothing cold or corporate).

## Brand feel
The client's words: طازة، قريب، واضح، إسكندراني، من غير فذلكة: fresh, close, clear, Alexandrian, no showing off. They like their kraft bags, old Alexandrian shop signs, and a counter where someone explains the coffee. They don't like black-and-gold luxury coffee, chain looks, unexplained snob jargon, fake urgency.
