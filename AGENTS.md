1. **Read [OWNER-NOTES.md](OWNER-NOTES.md) first,** then [OWNER-RULES.txt](OWNER-RULES.txt). Save the notes to your own memory if you have one.
2. **Log every note the owner gives you** in OWNER-NOTES.md in the same session (date, client, their words, how to apply it). Commit it and push that commit after checking no `clients/*/private/` file, contact detail or login is in it (owner rule 5). Everything else is pushed only when the owner says (rule 1).
3. **Never delete or trash a merchant's products or categories** on their store, for any reason (rule 6).
